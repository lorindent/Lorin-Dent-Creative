My name is Lorin Dent and I was born and raised in Decatur, Ga. I was first introduced to photography at a young age with my siblings assisting our father on photo shoots. I honestly didn't develop an interest in the crafting of still images until I discovered film-making. It was through making movies with my friends that I understood the power of visual story-telling.
